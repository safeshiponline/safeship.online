# SafeShip India: Brand Voice & Anti-AI Content Manifesto

## 1. The Core Philosophy
SafeShip is not an "innovative logistics aggregator leveraging paradigm-shifting synergy."
SafeShip is the **only courier delivery rail in India that lets you open, power on, and inspect secondhand electronics before paying a single rupee for the product.**

Every piece of content must sound like it was written by an ultra-sharp, street-smart Indian founder who has bought gadgets on OLX, seen friends get scammed with fake courier slips or soap bars, and built the exact bulletproof tool they wished existed.

---

## 2. Anti-AI Blacklist (Strictly Forbidden)
Never use these words, structures, or habits in any SafeShip post, thread, or video script:
- ❌ **Forbidden Clichés:** "delve", "testament", "tapestry", "game-changer", "unleash", "revolutionize", "seamlessly", "furthermore", "in conclusion", "it is important to remember", "beacon of trust", "paramount".
- ❌ **Forbidden Sentence Structures:**
  - "In today's digital era / fast-paced world..."
  - "Whether you are a buyer or a seller..."
  - "Look no further than..."
  - Starting every paragraph with a rhetorical question ("Have you ever wondered...?").
- ❌ **Forbidden Tone:** Customer-service robotic sweetness. We are not a bank toll center saying "We regret the inconvenience." We are assertive, direct, confident, and protective of the customer's hard-earned money.

---

## 3. The Authentic Indian Voice & Vocabulary
- **Conversational & Direct:** Talk like real people in Delhi, Bangalore, Mumbai, or Jaipur talking to a friend over chai.
- **Approved Power Phrases (Hindi / Hinglish & English):**
  - *"Samne khol ke on karke check karo, pasand aye tabhi payment do."*
  - *"₹0 advance for the product. Delivery boy ke samne test karo."*
  - *"COD is a trap. Courier boy parcel tab tak nahi deta jab tak cash na le le. Phir andar chahe brick nikle ya fake phone, courier company hath khade kar leti hai."*
  - *"Scammer ki sabse badi dushmani: Doorstep Open-Box Inspection."*
  - *"10-minute unhurried test window at your doorstep."*
  - *"RBI Section 10A trustee nodal account. Not random UPI."*
  - *"Toll-free 1800 890 2829. Real human support, not dumb bots."*
